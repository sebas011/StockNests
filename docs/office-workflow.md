# Office workflow

StockNest is for the **Office of the Vice President for Academic Affairs**. GAA and APPCSE keep separate budgets, requests and item allowances. This workflow replaces the original institution-wide proposal.

## Start with the approved annual items

Submit your annual projection to Supply using your existing office process. After external approval, add the approved items in **Annual projections**. Enter the approved quantity, estimated price and approval reference/details. Saving records an already approved plan; there is no second approval inside StockNest.

The annual allowance table shows approved, requested, received and remaining quantities for each item, fund and year. A later externally approved change is recorded as an amendment so the original stays available. An amendment cannot reduce an allowance below the quantity already requested.

## Request items from Supply

1. In **Item requests**, create a request and select **Request items from Supply**. A saved draft does not use the allowance.
2. Choose **Submit to Supply** when you submit the request through your normal Supply process. This records the submission and deducts its quantities from the annual allowance. StockNest does not send the document to Supply automatically.
3. Choose **Record Supply receipt** when items arrive. Partial deliveries are supported; enter the delivery/issuance reference and quantities actually received. Receiving increases office stock and reduces quantities awaiting delivery. It does not deduct the annual allowance a second time.

Example: 100 approved reams, 30 submitted and 20 received means **70 remaining to request**, **10 awaiting delivery** and **20 received**, before office consumption.

Requests exceeding the remaining allowance are rejected. Canceling an entirely unreceived request restores its allowance. A request with receipts cannot be canceled because that would erase the link to actual stock.

## Cash requests and outside purchases

When Supply cannot provide an item, choose **Cash request for outside purchase** and record the reason and estimated items. Submit the cash request through your existing external process. In StockNest, **Record cash request** makes its items available for recording an outside purchase; it does not certify that cash was approved.

After obtaining cash authorization, record the outside purchase with the supplier and **approved cash authority reference**. This reserves the estimated purchase amount against the selected fund's recorded budget. Record actual deliveries and payments separately under **Purchasing**. Final payment closes a fully received purchase and releases any unused commitment.

Outside requests and purchases have their own monthly totals. **They never reduce the Supply annual item allowance.** They still affect the selected fund's outside-purchase budget and office stock when received.

## Office inventory and physical counts

Use **Record office use** on a stock line to record items consumed or handed to staff. This reduces stock on hand, preserves a movement record and leaves the annual request allowance unchanged. Negative stock is prevented.

Physical counts keep a snapshot of stock at the displayed cutoff. Encode quantities and explain discrepancies, finalize the count, then explicitly reconcile. Reconciliation applies the variance to the current balance, preserving transactions after the cutoff. No separate internal approver is required.

## Monthly statistics and printing

The overview shows item quantities by activity month: Supply requests use the submission date, deliveries use their actual recording date, and outside cash requests and deliveries appear separately. The reports page exports the **annual Supply allowance** and **monthly item statistics**, alongside budgets, requests, purchases, stock, movements, counts and audit records, to CSV, Excel or PDF.

These exports are general reports. The official Supply and cash-request print layouts will be added after the office provides the form templates.

## What changed

The office identity is fixed; there is no department selector or department creation. The Approval Center, internal approval stages, Supply-side stock reservations, inter-location transfer workflow and request-based warehouse issuance are absent from the office screens. Accounts, fund permissions, budgets, catalog, purchasing, inventory, counts, reports and the audit trail remain.

Upgrades add fields and receipt tables without deleting historical documents. Existing records retain their original department and approval history. Newly encoded office plans and requests use OVPAA. Historical documents from the previous institutional workflow remain readable, but they are not automatically reclassified as this office's newly approved Supply allowance; encode the approved office baseline before creating new Supply requests.

For the portable download and updates, see the [Windows guide](operations/portable-windows.md). Each PC keeps separate records unless you move the stopped application's complete data folder.

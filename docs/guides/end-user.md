# End-user workflows

This guide describes the target StockNest workflow. Screens/actions become available as their modules are implemented; the root README records the delivered scope. Your available actions depend on your role, department, funding source and approval assignment.

## Common principles

Select the correct fiscal year and funding source before entering a document. GAA and APPCSE are separate funds even when they use the same item. Use the shared catalog item and its stated unit; do not substitute a different unit without an authorized conversion.

Save incomplete work as a draft. Submission locks a revision for review. Read validation messages and current status before retrying; repeated clicks must not create another transaction. Attach only relevant permitted documents. Provide clear purpose, variance or cancellation reasons.

Approval authorizes an action; it does not prove that goods moved or money was paid. Stock changes when an authorized physical transaction is confirmed. Spending appears when an explicit expenditure is posted. A physical count observes stock; reconciliation requires approval and a separate adjustment.

## Annual projection preparer

Choose year/fund/department, add catalog items, quantities, estimated unit prices, justification, priority and optional monthly breakdown. Check calculated totals, import validation results and duplicate lines. Submit to the required reviewers.

If returned, read comments, revise and resubmit. After approval, preserve the original baseline: use an amendment to change estimated quantities/prices, remove requirements or add new items. The original versus revised view explains how the plan evolved; it is not a payment report.

## Department requester

Create a monthly item request with fund, department, needed date, items/quantities and purpose. Check stock availability, estimated cost, fund budget and whether each item is planned. An unplanned item needs justification and the configured exception review.

Submit and follow the status timeline. Submitted requests remain pending until all required approval stages finish. If returned, revise/resubmit. After approval, track approved versus fulfilled quantities. Contact the assigned custodian/procurement staff through your organization's process; approval alone does not issue or order goods.

Use the controlled change/cancellation action if an approved requirement changes. Do not create a duplicate request to hide a mistaken quantity. Accept or acknowledge actual issued quantities, and return unused items against the original issuance.

## Department head and fund approver

Open the approval inbox and verify fund/department, exact document revision, purpose, planned/unplanned status, approved allocation, outstanding commitments, stock, attachments and previous decisions. Approve only within your active assigned authority. Use partial approval only where configured, with a clear authorized quantity.

Reject or return with a useful comment. Do not approve your own submission; route to an independently authorized approver. An old browser tab can refer to a stale revision; refresh/review the current document before deciding. An over-budget or unplanned exception requires its own configured authority.

## Procurement officer

Start from approved unmet request quantities. Create a procurement requisition, choose supplier and prepare the PO with exact quantities/prices/terms and supporting documents. Submit the order for approval; its authorized commitment is visible after approval, while physical stock remains unchanged.

Track ordered, accepted received, returned, canceled and outstanding quantities. Each delivery has its own goods receipt. Price/quantity changes need the controlled amendment route. Close or cancel only through the authorized action so unused commitments are released accurately. Supplier credits/refunds must link to separate financial processing; a physical return is not automatically a refund.

## Inventory custodian

For receiving, inspect the delivery against the approved PO, select actual location/lot/fund ownership, record accepted/rejected quantities and document reference, then confirm. Only accepted quantities increase stock. Record expiry/batch when used.

For fulfillment, choose an approved request, inspect available stock owned by the same fund, reserve/pick as needed and prepare issuance. Confirm actual release with recipient, issuer and quantity. Confirmation reduces stock and records fulfillment; draft/pick/reservation alone does not reduce on-hand. Partial issuance is valid when stock or recipient requirements warrant it.

Record unused returns against the original issuance, checking quantity and condition. Transfer only through an authorized source/destination transaction. Never alter an item master or a stock balance to correct physical stock. Use approved adjustment/reconciliation procedures.

## Physical inventory counter and reviewer

Use your assigned monthly location session and count sheet. Confirm its cutoff time, ownership labels and movement-control instructions. Count physical stock carefully; record quantity, observation time, reason for differences and any movements between cutoff and observation. Save incomplete work; record recounts as separate observations.

Submit when complete. A negative variance means fewer units than the cutoff system balance; a positive variance means more. Neither changes stock while waiting for review. The independent reviewer checks evidence/recounts and authorizes a linked adjustment. Reconciliation preserves later legitimate stock movements, so current stock may differ from the historical count after posting.

Example: cutoff balance 50, cutoff physical count 47 → variance −3. If five units are issued after cutoff, current stock is 45 before reconciliation and becomes 42 after the approved −3 adjustment. Reconciliation does not replace current stock with 47.

If movements or fund ownership cannot be established, document the issue and request review. Do not guess an allocation between GAA and APPCSE or force a negative stock result.

## Budget officer and auditor

Compare original plan, revised plan, authorized allocation, outstanding commitment, actual spending and available budget independently. Use fiscal year/fund/department/date filters and read report as-of metadata. Confirm your accounting recognition policy before interpreting receipts as expenses; the default requires explicit expenditure posting.

Trace an item from planning → request → approval → PO → receipt → issuance → expenditure as applicable; existing-stock fulfillment may have no new purchase. Trace each count variance to its independent approval and posted adjustment. Auditor access is read-only and scoped. Store exported reports securely and use authorized private download links.

## When an action is unavailable

Check account status, document state, department/fund scope and pending approval step. A visible record does not imply authority to act. Report an unexpected denial with its document reference and message to the administrator; do not bypass the workflow with duplicate records or another user's account.
